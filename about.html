About the Project

About Sunlytics

An AI-Based Solar Power Generation Prediction System built as a final year engineering project.

Project Objective

To design and deploy a machine learning system that accurately predicts solar power generation from real-time environmental and panel-condition data, enabling proactive energy planning.

Problem Statement

Solar power output varies with weather and panel conditions, making manual estimation unreliable. Sunlytics addresses this by learning patterns directly from historical operational data.

Dataset Information

The model is trained on a dataset of 10,000 solar installation records, capturing readings such as solar irradiance, panel and ambient temperature, cloud cover, humidity, wind speed, rainfall, dust level, panel efficiency, and inverter efficiency — with Solar Power Output as the target variable.

Workflow
Data Collection

Load the raw solar installation dataset.

Preprocessing

Drop identifier columns and engineer the Hour feature from timestamp data.

Exploratory Analysis

Distribution plots, correlation heatmaps and outlier detection.

Model Training

GridSearchCV-tuned Random Forest Regressor with 5-fold cross-validation.

Evaluation

R², MAE, MSE and RMSE on a held-out test set.

Deployment
